How to check a rationale

Each screen shows one rater's rationale and the criticisms a model pulled out of it. You are checking the extraction against the rationale text only. Do not judge the TORS response, and do not judge whether the rater was clinically right.

  1. Is it a criticism stated in the rationale? Yes if the rater actually makes this criticism. No if it is invented, is really praise, or repeats another item.
  2. Category correct? Tap the category name to see its definition.
  3. Domain correct? Accuracy, safety or relevance. "Unspecified" is correct when the rater gave no domain.
  4. Missed criticisms: count criticisms in the rationale that no item captures, and list them in a few words. Enter 0 (None) if nothing was missed. Text that is not highlighted is where missed criticisms hide.

Hedged or minor criticisms count ("slightly optimistic", "minor imprecision"). Statements that something was done appropriately are praise, not criticism.

Your answers save automatically and only you can see them. Work independently; do not discuss until both checkers have finished. Start with the Practice set to get used to the task; it is not part of the study sample.

On a phone, add this page to your Home Screen (Safari: Share, then Add to Home Screen) to open it like an app.
